=====================================================================

=====================================================================
6. LOANS (new)
=====================================================================
Bank screen has a Loan card. All settings are in CONFIG.loan:
- Borrow in $50 steps. Credit limit = $1,000 + 2 x (cash + savings + investments),
  never more than $5,000. The limit and its formula are shown on the card.
- Choose 3, 5 or 10 days: 4%, 5% or 6% simple interest a day on the principal you
  still owe, only for the days you keep the loan. Repay any time, in part or in
  full, from CASH only (interest first, then principal).
- After the due date the whole balance grows 8% a day, compounding.
- LOAN DEFAULT (game over) if you owe more than $10,000, or have not repaid in
  full 5 days after the due date.
- One loan at a time; you cannot borrow again until it is paid off.
- What you owe is subtracted from net worth, so borrowing does not inflate score.
- The card shows the amount borrowed, what you owe now (principal + interest),
  the due date and any overdue warning. Morning notes and the warnings list track
  the due date. Rules screen explains loans and loan default.
- Arranging a loan takes 15 minutes. Interest is rounded to whole dollars a night.


=====================================================================
7. BANKRUPT RULE
=====================================================================
Old: net worth of -$200 or lower ended the game at once.
New (CONFIG.bankrupt): net worth under $500 at the end of 3 days in a row, counted
from day 5 (fromDay = 5; the earliest bankruptcy is the end of day 7).
Warnings appear after the first and second day. Note: counted from day 1 this
ended about 60% of simulated games on day 4, which is why a start day was added.

11. ECONOMY, EVENTS AND HEALTH (latest round)
=====================================================================
Savings and investments now follow the economy (loan stays fixed):
- Every macro shock and storyline now moves the savings rate (before, only about
  half of them did). Examples: wars, tightening, inflation, currency crises push it
  UP; QE, pandemics, housing crashes, deflation, financial crises push it DOWN.
- Fixed deposit follows the savings rate (rate x 1.4). ETF, hedge fund and crypto
  follow the market mood, and are now also pulled down when interest is above its
  normal level (and lifted when below): CONFIG.market.rateBeta
  (ETF 0.3, hedge fund 0.4, crypto 0.6 per point of interest above normal).
- With no news pushing interest, the rate now drifts back to normal by itself
  (CONFIG.market.rateBase = 1.0, rateReversion = 10% of the gap each morning).
  Before, a rate moved by news stayed there for ever.
- The Bank screen has a news line: which story is moving rates and markets, the
  market mood, and that loan rates are fixed. Savings and investment cards say
  their return moves with the economy.
- Loan rates (CONFIG.loan) are never touched by news. Checked over 740 simulated
  days: the loan rate never changed.

End-of-day events vs your stats:
- Audited 1,500 random end-of-day events (news, decisions, stories, storylines,
  repairs, daily events). The cash, happiness, energy and knowledge numbers shown
  on the event matched the real change in every case.
- Gap found: NO event could change health (there was no health effect at all).
  Added a health effect ("+2 health") and used it on health-themed outcomes:
  gym membership (+3), masks and sanitiser (+2 / -2 if you skip), hospitals
  overwhelmed (-2), vaccine approved (+3).

"I slept all day but health stayed at 80":
- Not a bug in the sleep itself. Sleeping at home gives +10 health, but the night
  also costs -1 for everyone and -10 if you did not eat that day: +10 -1 -10 = -1.
  Fed and sleeping at home you gain +9 (tested: 80 to 89); unfed, 80 to 79.
- The game never told you. Now: going to bed unfed shows a warning, the log shows
  "Overnight health +9 (-1 every night, +10 slept at home)" with every reason, and
  the morning recap has a new health figure.

SLEEP REWORK (latest):
- Sleeping at the apartment: energy is 3% per hour left, capped at +35 (was +75).
  Health is +1 per hour left, capped at +10 (was a flat +10). Happiness is still
  +1 per full hour left.
- Sleeping anywhere else: health, happiness and energy are all HALF of what the
  apartment would give (CONFIG.sleep.awayShare = 0.5; before only energy was
  reduced, to 25% with a cap of 15, and health and happiness were zero).
  Examples: 8h left = home +24% energy, +8 health, +8 happy; outside +12, +4, +4.
  16h or more left = home +35, +10, +16/+24; outside +18, +5, +8/+12.
- Settings: CONFIG.sleep.maxEnergy, awayShare; CONFIG.health.sleep.perHour and max.
  CONFIG.sleep.awayMax and CONFIG.health.nightly.sleptAtHome were removed.
  Night Owl trait now sets awayShare to 0.75 ("helps 50% more").
- The top-bar "End day" button reads "Sleep outside" whenever you are not at home.
  At home it stays "End day" (and the apartment has its own Sleep button).
- Going to bed unfed warns that the -10 health penalty eats into the sleep bonus.
