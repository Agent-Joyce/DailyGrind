Daily Grind QA Report

Build tested: index.html with the uncommitted bank, school and qualification changes. 2,000 simulated games across 79 job roles and 3 difficulties, plus 225 text checks and 857 button checks.

The short version
  • Buttons: every button works. One control is permanently hidden (the bottom-sheet toggle), and one bug makes the game unplayable (below).
  • Text vs game logic: 217 of 225 checks match. The biggest gap is pay: the screen shows base pay and the game pays up to 25% more.
  • 2,000 games: 91.2% reached day 15 or Tycoon. 175 ended early (8.8%): 161 from the bot skipping meals on purpose, 13 from a game bug, 1 from an unpaid hospital bill. One more game froze for good (same bug).
  • Winning is easy: 91.2% of games reached the $2,000 win line, usually on day 5.
  • Best way to win: study one short pathway (4–32 hours of lessons) in the first days, then work every hour at a job paying $200–$249 a shift. Details in section 4.

3. The 2,000-game stress test

How the simulated player behaves

A script plays the real game through its own buttons, like a careful human. It eats every day, buys clothes the day before they wear out, and deposits spare cash. Each game targets a different role (79 roles, about 25 games each, spread evenly over Easy, Normal and Hard). It studies that role’s qualifications one pathway at a time, and always works the best-paying job per hour it qualifies for. To match “in most cases”, it also has random slips: on any day there is a 2% chance it skips meals for 1–3 days and a 4% chance it forgets to buy clothes for 1–3 days.

What to do

  1. Treat time as the only currency. Winners worked about 16 hours a day. Keeping 8 hours free for sleep halved the score (4,294 vs 9,321) and pushed the $2,000 line from day 5 to day 12. Two shifts a day: 4,779.
  2. Study one short pathway in the first 1–4 days, then stop. A Certificate (4 hours of lessons) or a single Degree (28 hours) scored 8,681–8,935. Skipping school scored 7,414 (-1,901 against baseline). Honours and Masters pathways scored 7,047 and 7,152: none was finished in 15 days.
  3. Aim for the top of a pay band, not the bottom of the next. Shifts get longer at $130, $250, $350, $450 and $650, so hourly pay peaks just below each step. The top three were IT Support Lead ($240; Technology Degree + Engineering Certificate · Knowledge 20; 10,162 points), Technician ($180; Engineering Certificate + Technology Certificate; 10,026) and Area Manager ($220; Food & Beverage Degree + Finance Certificate · Knowledge 20; 9,807).
  4. Eat every day, one meal is enough. Two meals a day scored the same as one (+52). Three days without a meal ends the game; it did so in 160 games.
  5. Keep clothes valid. Replace them the day before they expire, matching the job’s dress code (smart casual for most mid-level roles). A job you cannot dress for pays nothing.
  6. Bank spare cash. Never banking cost 433 points a game.
  7. Hold at least $250 in cash until day 5 (more on Hard). It protects you from the Rent-increase freeze and from a hospital bill you cannot pay.
  8. Skip the long careers. The three lowest roles (Branch Manager, Investment Associate, Hospital Administrator) need 60–108 hours of study and were reached in 12% of games on average.

Why pay per hour peaks just below each step

Shift length depends on pay, so hourly pay (before the knowledge bonus) drops when a job crosses a step.

6. Method and limits

The page was loaded in headless Chromium. The bot reads the game’s state to decide, but acts only by clicking the real buttons. Every game uses a seeded random generator so any result can be replayed. For speed, two cosmetic functions were disabled in the test copy (keyboard-number badges and scroll-to-top); no rules were changed. The bot is one play style, tuned to be fast and careful; a human who sleeps, explores or misclicks will score lower. “Win” means net worth of $2,000 or more, the first rank. Nine games that first stalled were bot errors (it clicked a disabled End day button), fixed and re-run; the one remaining freeze is the game bug above. Raw results for all 2,000 games are in daily_grind_2000_runs.csv in the session scratch folder.
