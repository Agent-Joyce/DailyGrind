Daily log

Welcome to Daily Grind

Build a life from scratch. Reach $1,000 net worth without going broke, burning out or skipping meals.

  • Each day has 24 hours. Walking (5 minutes per building), working, studying and shopping all use time. Click Sleep in Apartment (at home) or Sleep outside (anywhere else) when you are done.
  • Eat once a day and keep an eye on energy and happiness. Low energy makes you ill; low happiness cuts your pay.
  • Rent is due every 5 days. If your cash is negative when it is due, you are evicted.
  • Start simple: you begin with Casual Clothes that last 5 days. Get a job at the Employment Office, work shifts, then study for better jobs. Replace your clothes at the Clothes Mall before they wear out.

The Getting started checklist on the main panel will guide your first days. Your game saves automatically.

How the game ends

You win

  • Net worth reaches $1,000: cash + bank + investments + the resale value of your things, minus hospital debt, rent arrears and any loan you owe, within 15 days. That makes you 🙂 Comfortable and you have won.
  • Keep playing for a higher rank: 💼 Wealthy at $5,000 and 👑 Tycoon at $10,000 (Tycoon ends the game). When time runs out, your best rank is your final result.
  • Reach a rank by its target day for bonus points.

You lose

  • Bankrupt: net worth under $0 for three days in a row.
  • Loan default: you owe more than $10,000, or have not repaid your loan in full 5 days after its due date.
  • Exhaustion: energy at 10% or less at the end of two days in a row.
  • You gave up: happiness at 10 or less for three days in a row.
  • Hospitalised: three days in a row without a meal.
  • Time’s up: Day 15 passes and you are still under $1,000.

Stress that builds before then

  • Every night: energy −15% and happiness −10, then sleep adds energy, happiness and health back for each hour you had left (more at home).
  • Tired (energy under 30): shifts pay 15% less. Low energy that lasts also raises your chance of falling ill overnight: about 40% a night under 11 energy (before health, diet and gym visits lower it).
  • Morale (happiness under 40 / 25 / 20): shifts pay 10% / 25% / 40% less, and below 20 your boss may demote or fire you.
  • Hunger: skipping the daily meal costs 20% energy and 15 happiness, and cuts productivity.
  • Money shame: checked when you end the day: net worth under $100 then costs 3 happiness, under $0 costs 8 happiness and 5% energy. Nothing is taken during the day.
  • Keyboard: 1–9 press the numbered buttons, E ends the day, Esc leaves a building or closes a window, H opens these rules, M toggles sound. Tab moves between buildings.
  • Health: a slow-moving vital. Healthy meals, the gym and sleeping at home raise it; hunger, illness, junk food, long study days and burnout lower it. Good health, a healthy diet and regular gym visits all cut your chance of falling ill.
  • Layoffs and closures (two different things): in a bad economy you can be laid off (the job is gone, so apply for a new one) or your workplace can close for 1 to 4 days (the Work button is disabled, but the job stays on your record and the morning message tells you when it is back). You can only hold one job at a time.
  • Loans: Borrow at the Bank in $50 steps.
  • Work limit: you can work at most 12 hours of shifts in one day.
  • Burnout: studying more than 5 hours a day (school plus self-study), or working 9 hours or more a day, three days in a row costs 15 health and cuts shift pay by 20% for 3 days.
  • Illness: a cold, the flu, dengue or pneumonia. While sick you cannot work. Hospital treatment always cures you (usually after a full day in the ward). The bill is charged the morning after you are released; whatever your cash cannot cover goes on a repayment plan (+10% interest, late fees on missed instalments), like rent arrears.
  • Housing bubble: when a housing bubble bursts you may lose your home.
  • School: while a qualification is in progress, 3 days without a lesson is a warning and 5 to 6 is expulsion. Once you have finished one and not started the next, nothing is skipped.
